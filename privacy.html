QTakeSync privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026.

QTakeSync is a macOS app that runs on the user's own Macs. It has no servers of its own. Nothing the app handles is sent to the developers of QTakeSync.

What the app accesses

  • Files on the Mac. QTake project folders and video clips on the user's Macs, to protect them, copy them between the user's Macs on the local network, and upload them.
  • Google Drive (only if the user connects it). With the drive.file scope the app can create folders and upload files, and read or change only the files and folders it created. It reads the account's email address and permission ID to show which account is connected. It cannot see any other file in the user's Drive.
  • Yandex Disk (only if the user connects it), with access limited to what is needed to upload files and check them.

Where data goes

Clips are uploaded directly from the user's Mac to the user's own Google Drive or Yandex Disk account. The sign-in tokens for these services are stored on the user's Mac, readable only by the system service of QTakeSync, and are never sent anywhere else. The app does not collect analytics, does not track users, does not sell or share data, and does not use data from Google APIs for advertising or to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

QTakeSync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnecting a cloud in QTakeSync deletes the stored sign-in on that Mac. Google access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing QTakeSync's service from a Mac deletes all stored sign-ins on it. Files already uploaded stay in the user's cloud account until the user deletes them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at the project's public page on GitHub, github.com/myplacealreadyowned/qtakesync-site/issues.
